About Jake

Jake Hulsh is a Senior Associate at BKREA, where he works alongside Bob Knakal advising owners of multifamily, mixed-use, and development properties across New York City on sale strategy and value.

At BKREA, Jake has represented sellers on transactions including 500 Columbus Avenue, 10 West 17th Street, 11 East 78th Street, 57 East 55th Street (the historic Friars Club building), 42 Second Avenue, and 8-10 Maiden Lane.

Before real estate, Jake spent five and a half years in property technology. He was an early employee at VERO Leasing, where he helped build products that streamlined leasing for landlords and renters, and later led partnership strategy at Obligo, establishing alliances with institutional property management platforms including RealPage and AppFolio.

Jake is active in NYC investment sales, land, and development site transactions. See his deal history on Traded.
